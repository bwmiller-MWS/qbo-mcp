Set up MWS QuickBooks in Claude

About 5 minutes. No technical knowledge needed.

You'll need: Claude Desktop installed and signed in with your work account, your own QuickBooks Online login, and the two codes Brian sends you (a Client ID and a Client Secret).

1Install the extension

  1. Download mws-qbo-mcp-<version>.mcpb from the shared QuickBooks Connector folder.
  2. Double-click the file. Claude Desktop opens an install window. Click Install.
Nothing happens when you double-click? In Claude Desktop, open Settings → Extensions, then drag the file onto that page (or use the install option there and choose the file).

2Fill in the settings

LIVE Client IDPaste the Client ID Brian sent you.
LIVE Client SecretPaste the Client Secret. Claude stores it securely.
Sandbox Client ID / SecretLeave blank unless Brian asks you to test in the sandbox.
Read-only modeLeave off unless Brian tells you otherwise. When it's on, Claude can look things up but can't change anything in QuickBooks.
Sign-in relay addressLeave as is.

Click Save and make sure the extension is switched on.

3Connect your QuickBooks company

  1. Start a new chat in Claude and type: Connect a QuickBooks company
  2. Your browser opens Intuit's sign-in page. Sign in with your own QuickBooks login.
  3. Choose the company (entity) and click Connect.
  4. When the page says "Connected to QuickBooks", close that tab and go back to Claude. Say done.

Repeat for each company you work in. Claude remembers them all.

If Intuit says you don't have permission to connect apps, ask a QuickBooks company admin, or Brian, for help.

4Use it

Just ask in plain English. For example:

  • Show me September's P&L by class
  • Which invoices are overdue, and who owes the most?
  • What's in OFFICE expenses this month?
  • Find transactions with no class this month
  • Which companies are connected? · Switch to <company name> · Switch to sandbox
Before you click Allow: Claude asks your permission before every change it makes in QuickBooks (new invoices, bills, customers, imports). Check the company name and the details first. Every change says which company it went to, and whether that's the LIVE books or the SANDBOX test company.

5Updates

When Brian announces a new version, download the new .mcpb file and double-click it. Your connections and settings are kept, so there's nothing else to do. To check your version, ask Claude: Which QuickBooks connector version am I using?
