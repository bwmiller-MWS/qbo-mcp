Connect or reconnect to QuickBooks

MWS QBO MCP runs on your own computer, so you connect it from there:

  1. On the computer where MWS QBO MCP is installed, open PowerShell in the app's folder.
  2. Run npm run auth.
  3. Your browser opens Intuit's sign-in page. Sign in and choose the Middle West Spirits company.
  4. When you see "Connected to QuickBooks", restart Claude Desktop.

If the app finds it has been disconnected, it also opens the Intuit sign-in page automatically.
