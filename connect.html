Connect or reconnect to QuickBooks

MWS QBO MCP runs on your own computer, so you connect it from there, in Claude Desktop:

  1. Start a new chat and type: Connect a QuickBooks company (or connect a sandbox company).
  2. Your browser opens Intuit's sign-in page. A QuickBooks admin must sign in (Intuit only lets admins connect apps). Choose the company, then the admin signs out of Intuit in that browser.
  3. When you see "Connected to QuickBooks", go back to Claude and say done.

Finish within 15 minutes. If the browser shows "localhost refused to connect", the time ran out; just ask Claude to connect again. New to MWS QBO MCP? Start with the install guide.
