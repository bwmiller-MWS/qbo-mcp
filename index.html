MWS QBO MCP

Middle West Spirits' internal connector between QuickBooks Online and Claude.

You're connected. MWS QBO MCP lets authorized Middle West Spirits staff ask Claude Desktop questions about the company's QuickBooks Online books and, with their approval, record customers, invoices, bills, and transactions.

How to use it

Open Claude Desktop on the computer where the app is installed and ask a question, for example "Show me last month's P&L" or "Which invoices are overdue?". Claude will ask for your approval before it makes any change in QuickBooks.
